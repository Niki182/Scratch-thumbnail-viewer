Welcome to the Scratch Thumbnail Viewer! This website allows you to view the thumbnails of Scratch projects and studios by entering their respective IDs. You can navigate to the "Project thumbnail" or "Studio thumbnail" pages using the links above to get started.

How this website works

This website uses uploads.scratch.mit.edu to get the thumbnails of Scratch projects and studios. Please note that the thumbnails are dynamically generated and may not always be available for all projects or studios.
